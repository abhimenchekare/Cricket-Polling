Before the event
Clear it out

Pick the smallest thing that fixes your problem. You will be asked to type CLEAR before anything is deleted, and your admin password, event name and round length are never touched.

1 · Clear rounds & guesses

Deletes every round, every guess and every sold price. Players go back to the top of the queue.

Keeps: your auction list, participants, settings.

2 · Clear audience too

Everything above, and every participant is removed — for when rehearsal phones joined with test names and you want the room to join fresh with their real ones.

Keeps: your auction list, settings.

3 · Delete everything

A completely empty app: no rounds, no guesses, no participants and no auction list. Use this only if you want to paste a brand-new player list from scratch.

Keeps: your admin account and password.

Same thing from a terminal: npm run reset -- --yes (local database only).
